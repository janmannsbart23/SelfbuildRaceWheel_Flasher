This browser cannot access serial devices. Please use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial). Firefox and Safari do not support it.

The normal way to update. The PC talks to the wheel through the USB dongle and its radio link (ESP-NOW). The wheel ESP32, the wheel's STM32 and the display are updated one after the other; each device restarts on its own when it is done.

1

Connect the dongle

Plug the USB dongle into the PC and switch the wheel on. Close SimHub first (or disconnect its "Custom Serial Device") – otherwise the COM port is busy.

2

Update a device

Devices appear as soon as their heartbeat arrives. "In bootloader" means a previous update was interrupted – the device keeps waiting and can simply be flashed again.

1

What should be installed?

For new or empty boards, for the dongle, or when a device can no longer be reached through the dongle. Everything is written: ESP32 bootloader, partition table, UDS bootloader and the application.

2

Connect via USB and install

  1. Connect the board directly to the PC with a USB cable (wheel: USB port of the ESP32; dongle: plug it in).
  2. Click "Connect & install" and choose the port.
  3. If the connection fails: hold BOOT, press RESET briefly (or plug in), release BOOT – then try again.
